About Us

Started under a banyan tree in 2019

Meera Iyer, a retired schoolteacher, began with eleven children and a borrowed blackboard in Kolad. Their parents worked the fields until dark, and the government school was two buses away.

Today the same evening class runs in 18 centres, taught by local volunteers who finished Class 12 and want to stay in the village. We train them, pay them a stipend and visit every centre each fortnight.

We are a registered public charitable trust. Our accounts and learning results are published every June.

  • Reading firstEvery other subject depends on it, so every centre starts there.
  • Teachers from the villageChildren learn better from someone they see at the well the next morning.
  • Measure and publishWe test each child twice a year and share the results, good or poor.

Services

Four programmes, one goal

  • Class 1 to 5

    Evening Learning Centres

    Two hours of reading, writing and arithmetic in small groups of fifteen, using picture books in Marathi and simple English.

    WhenMon to Sat, 5:00 to 7:00 pm
  • Class 6 to 10

    Digital Literacy Lab

    Twelve refurbished laptops running offline lessons. Students learn typing, spreadsheets and how to fill in a scholarship form.

    WhenSat and Sun, 10:00 am to 1:00 pm
  • Girls, Class 8+

    Stay-in-School Scholarship

    Yearly support for uniforms, bus passes and exam fees, paired with a mentor who meets the family twice a term.

    ApplicationsOpen every April
  • For volunteers

    Teacher Training

    A six-day course on teaching early reading, then monthly classroom visits and a peer group where teachers swap what worked.
